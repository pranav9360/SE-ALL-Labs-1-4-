**Project:** Patient Health Record Consent Management System (Labs 1–3)

This repository consolidates the available deliverables for Software Engineering Labs 1–4. The original Lab 2 and Lab 4 repositories remain unchanged.

## Lab folders

| Lab | Folder | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| **Lab 1 — Requirements** | `Lab-1-Requirements/` | Requirements table in Markdown, two Word table documents, and the UML use-case diagram. |

## Project overview

The Patient Health Record Consent Management System is designed to manage patient consent for medical record access, including granting and revoking consent, selecting records, verifying requesters, validating consent, and maintaining audit records.
